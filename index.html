Guilds of Hyppos - Necromancer Selection Bug

Reproduction

  1. Have Necromancer + skeleton/minion + pet near an item on the ground.
  2. Click the item on the ground.
  3. Box-select the hero together with skeleton/minion/pet.
  4. Hero appears selected as the main unit.
  5. Hero spells become unusable. Both hotkeys and manual spell clicks fail.
  6. Movement and attack still work.
  7. The issue resets by single-selecting the hero, box-selecting only the hero, pressing Tab back to the hero, or waiting for skeletons to expire.

Suspected cause

This looks like a command-card or selection-state bug caused by selecting a ground item before box-selecting Necromancer together with summons.
